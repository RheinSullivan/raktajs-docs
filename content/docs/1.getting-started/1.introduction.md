Rakta.js is a lightweight, composable frontend framework built on top of the Bun runtime. It pairs a familiar React-based component model with a file-based routing layer, so you spend your time building routes instead of wiring up plumbing. 

Most frameworks force you to choose between a minimal frontend-only setup and a full backend stack from day one. Rakta.js lets you start frontend-only and scale into a fullstack project only when your product actually needs one.
